15:T1487,<pre style="text-align: center;"><b>WATER BAPTISM</b></pre>
<h6><b>Unto what Name were you baptized? Were you baptized using the Titles of Father, Son and Holy Ghost or using the Name of Jesus Christ? Is there any difference between the two?</b></h6>
<h6><b>WATER BAPTISM </b><span style="font-weight: 400;">is a basic requirement to a Christian’s salvation. </span><b>MARK 16:16 </b><span style="font-weight: 400;">states, “</span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">He that believeth and is baptized shall be SAVED; but he that believeth NOT</span></i> <i><span style="font-weight: 400;">shall be damned.</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;">” </span><b>I Peter 3:21 </b><span style="font-weight: 400;">also reads, “</span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">The like figure whereunto [even] baptism doth</span></i> <i><span style="font-weight: 400;">also now SAVE us (not the putting away of the filth of the flesh, but the answer of a good</span></i> <i><span style="font-weight: 400;">conscience toward God,) by the resurrection of Jesus Christ</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;">.”</span></h6>
<h6><span style="font-weight: 400;">A series of questions usually arise in Water Baptism: “What is the Biblical way of doing it?” Is baptism performed by sprinkling? Or is it by immersion in water? Is it using the TITLES of Father, Son and Holy Ghost (Matt. 28:19), or using the NAME of the Lord Jesus Christ? (Acts 2:38)?</span></h6>
<h6><span style="font-weight: 400;">The Bible cannot contradict itself for it is the Word of God. What we need today is a revelation from Almighty God in order for us to understand and dovetail every word in it correctly. Let us therefore take a closer look upon this all-time controversial issue.</span></h6>
<h6><b>The word “baptize” originated from the Greek Word “</b><b><i>baptizo</i></b><b>” which means to “IMMERSE” in water.</b></h6>
<h6><span style="font-weight: 400;">For the first thirteen hundred years from the death of Christ, baptism was always performed by IMMERSION of the person UNDER WATER. </span><b>Acts 8:38-39 </b><span style="font-weight: 400;">states this, “</span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">And he commanded the chariot to stand still: </span></i><b><i>and they went DOWN both INTO the WATER, both Philip and the Eunuch; and</i></b> <b><i>he baptized him. And when they were came up OUT OF THE WATER</i></b><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">, the Spirit of the Lord caught away Philip, that the eunuch saw him no more: and went on his way rejoicing”</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;">. </span><b>John 3:23 </b><span style="font-weight: 400;">also states, “</span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">John also was</span></i> <i><span style="font-weight: 400;">baptizing at Aenon near to Salim, because there was MUCH WATER</span></i> <i><span style="font-weight: 400;">there : and they came, and were baptised</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;">”.</span></h6>
<h6><span style="font-weight: 400;">It is therefore evident from these very scriptures that Water Baptism was originally performed by “immersion” of the individual person underwater.</span></h6>
<h6><b>TITLES OR NAME?</b></h6>
<h6><span style="font-weight: 400;">In Matthew 28:19 we can read Jesus’ great commission unto His disciples saying,</span></h6>
<h6><span style="font-weight: 400;">“Go ye therefore, and teach all nations, </span><b>BAPTIZING them in the NAME </b><span style="font-weight: 400;">of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost.” Now, Jesus Christ, the Author and Finisher of our faith, never told us to baptize in the NAMES, but in the N-A-M-E, (singular). The clue here is to find what NAME the Father, the Son, and the Holy Ghost possess. Take note that the word </span><b>“Father” is not a NAME.</b></h6>
<h6><b>“Son” is not a NAME either; neither is the Holy Ghost a NAME. These are but TITLES of the ONE true GOD.</b></h6>
<h6><span style="font-weight: 400;">We can now therefore conclude from these basis that </span><b>the TITLES of “Father, Son and Holy Ghost” refer to only one compound NAME</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">, which</span><b> is the only Name which was given on earth whereby man must be saved</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> (Acts 4:10-12) &#8211; and that NAME is</span> <span style="font-weight: 400;">nothing else but the NAME of the “</span><b>LORD JESUS CHRIST</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">”, the complete NAME of GOD.</span></h6>
<h6><span style="font-weight: 400;">We can read all throughout the Bible that ALL of Jesus’ disciples used His NAME in Water </span><b>Baptism (References: Acts 2: 38; Acts 19:1-5; Acts 10:46-48; Acts 8:14- 16)</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> .</span></h6>
<h6><span style="font-weight: 400;">NEVER can we find in the entire pages of the Bible where anybody was ever baptized using the TITLES of Father, Son and Holy Ghost. Look it up for yourself and you’ll NEVER find one.</span></h6>
