<p>Peter having the keys to the kingdom said, “Repent and be BAPTIZED EVERYONE OF YOU in the name of Jesus Christ for the REMISSION of SIN. &#8211; (ACTS 2:38) Since BAPTISM is important to salvation and that IMMERSION and not SPRINKLING is the BIBLE method, don’t you think you should be baptized right Before is too late?</p>
