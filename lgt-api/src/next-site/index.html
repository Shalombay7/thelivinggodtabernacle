Pastor's Welcome

The Living God Tabernacle Leadership

You belong here. We are building a Christ-centered family where children, youth, adults, and new believers can grow with confidence and hope.

We want every visitor to feel seen, safe, and encouraged from the moment they arrive.

Our Mission

Built for worship, discipleship, and spiritual family.

To raise wholehearted believers who know Christ, live His Word, and shine His love in every generation.

Prayer-Filled

Every gathering is shaped to feel reverent, expectant, and close to the presence of God.

Family-Centered

Children, youth, adults, and new believers are welcomed with dignity and pastoral warmth.

Word-Anchored

Teaching, discipleship, and encouragement stay rooted in Scripture and daily transformation.

What We Believe

“We believe in salvation through Jesus Christ, the authority of Scripture, the power of prayer, and the work of the Holy Spirit in the Church.”

Our theology is not treated as abstract language. It is meant to shape a lived culture of prayer, compassion, holiness, and joyful service.
