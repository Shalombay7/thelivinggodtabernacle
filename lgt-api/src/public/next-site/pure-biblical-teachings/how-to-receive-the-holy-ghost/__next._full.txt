<h6><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Now when they heard this, they were pricked in their heart, and said unto Peter and to the rest of the apostles Men and brethren, </span><b>what shall we do</b><b>?</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Then Peter said unto them, </span><b>Repent,</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> and be </span><b>baptized </b><span style="font-weight: 400;">every one of you </span><b>in</b> <b>the name</b> <b>of Jesus Christ</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> for the remission of sins, </span><b>and ye shall receive the gift of the Holy Ghost.</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Acts 2:37-38</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> </span></h6>
<h6><span style="font-weight: 400;">       Stage 1. </span><b>REPENTANCE:</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> “repentance toward God” </span></h6>
<h6><span style="font-weight: 400;">What do we repent for? Remission of Sin </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">(From the dictionary “Remission” is a pardon of a sin, the forgiveness of an offence</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;">).that is taking away of our past sin, before coming to the knowledge of truth (Acts 17:30) not the future sin and not Adam’s sin, what we did is chopped off but our </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">old nature is still there.</span></i></h6>
<h6><span style="font-weight: 400;">llustration:</span></h6>
<h6><span style="font-weight: 400;">    </span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Fig .1                                                                                                                                                                                                   Fig.2</span></h6>
<h6><span style="font-weight: 400;">  <img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="wp-image-333 alignright" src="https://thelivinggodtabernacle.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/Heart-Dove.jpg" alt="" width="407" height="363" />           <img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="wp-image-334 alignleft" src="https://thelivinggodtabernacle.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/Heart-Snake.jpg" alt="" width="405" height="361" />                                                                                                                </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">    </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">                       </span></h6>
<h6><span style="font-weight: 400;">   </span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> NOTE:</span><b> Snake = Sin         Dove = Holy Spirit</b></h6>
<h6><span style="font-weight: 400;">(Above are two hearts of human, inside one is </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">a </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">snake</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;"> the other is </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">a </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">dove</span></i><i><span style="font-weight: 400;"> as life in each</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;">. Now when we are forgiven of our sin, we are done away with the malice hatred, etc. but the thing that made us do them are still there. That is </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">the old root of evil it’s still there)</span></i></h6>
<h6><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">    Stage 2.</span></i><b> WATER BAPTISM:</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> that is to be immersed into water in the name of the Lord Jesus Christ to cleanse the mind and sets the heart in order of holiness. A man repents of sins (eg. Immoral, drunkard, etc.) and any time he sees any immoral looking woman he is still thinking of  it ,why ? Drunkard any time he smells the drink he is still itching for it, why? Because the old root of evil is still there. But then when he/she gets sanctified, that cleanses that desire out of him/her. it takes the want of it away. Though he/she can still be tempted, but the want is taken away. Still, he is not right yet.</span><b> (Acts 2: 38; Acts 19:1-5; Acts 10:46-48; Acts 8:14-16)</b></h6>
<h6><span style="font-weight: 400;">   Stage 3.</span><b> Finally comes the baptism of THE HOLY GHOST, AND FIRE (Matt. 3:11), cleansed, burnt out cleaned up and then put you into service of God</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> . ( Ref;The Future home Of The Heavenly Bridegroom and The Earthly Bride, 64-0802 Vol.3-5</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">R</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Paragraph 87-98)</span></h6>
