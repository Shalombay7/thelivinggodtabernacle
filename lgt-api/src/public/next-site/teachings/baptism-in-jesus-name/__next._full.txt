									<h1 style="text-align: center;"><strong>BAPTISM IN JESUS NAME</strong></h1>
<h2 style="text-align: center;"><strong>According To The Bible</strong></h2>
<h5><strong>JESUS TAUGHT – </strong>“The REPENTACE AND REMISSION of sins should be preached in HIS NAME beginning at Jerusalem.”</h5>
<h5>(Luke 24:47)</h5>
<h5> </h5>
<h5><strong>PETER OBEYED –</strong> “REPENT and be BAPTIZED everyone of you in the NAME OF JESUS CHRIST for the REMISSION of SINS.”</h5>
<h5>(Acts 2:38-39)</h5>
<h5> </h5>
<h5><strong>SAMARITANS &#8211; </strong>“They were BAPTIZED … in the NAME of the LORD JESUS.”</h5>
<h5>(Acts 8:16)</h5>
<h5> </h5>
<h5><strong>BAPTISM COMMANDED – </strong>“He COMMANDED them to be BAPTIZED in the Name of the LORD JESUS.”</h5>
<h5>(RV) (Acts 10:48)</h5>
<h5> </h5>
<h5><strong>PAUL RE-BAPTIZED</strong> – “When they heard this they were BAPTIZED in the NAME of the LORD JESUS.”</h5>
<h5>(Acts 19:3-5)</h5>
<h5> </h5>
<h5><strong>EVRYTHING DONE IN JESUS NAME</strong> &#8211; “Whatsoever you do in word or in deed do ALL in the NAME of the LORD JESUS.”</h5>
<h5>(Col.3:17)</h5>
<h5> </h5>
<h5><strong>NO OTHER NAME FOR SALVATION</strong> &#8211; “There is NONE OTHER NAME under Heaven whereby we must be saved.”</h5>
<h5>(Acts 4:10-12)</h5>
<h5> </h5>
<h5><strong>SAVE BY BAPTISM</strong> &#8211; “BAPTISM doth also now SAVE us. The answer (response) of a good (repented) conscience towards God.”</h5>
<h5>(1Peter 3:21)</h5>
<p>The above Scriptures are not given to refute Matt.28:19, where Jesus told Apostles to baptize in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost. They merely show how the command was interpreted and obeyed by them.</p>
<p>The Apostles knew what the religious leaders fail to recognize. First: that the LORD JESUS CHRIST is the .FAMILY NAME. <u>(Eph.3:15)</u>  Second: That FULLNESS of the GODHEAD (Deity or God) dwelleth bodily in Christ<u>. (Col. 2:9)</u> Third: <u>That baptism must be performed in the name of the one crucified. (1Cor. 1:13)</u>*</p>
<p>They knew the name of the Son was Jesus. <u>(Matt. 1:21)</u>. They knew that the Son came in the Father’s name.(ST John 5:43). They also knew that the Holy Ghost was the Spirit of Christ and would come in Jesus name. <u>(ST. John 14:26)</u>.</p>
<p>The name JESUS means JEHOVAH SALVATION (Fausets Bible Encyclopedia, p. 359)</p>
